Common questions

Quick answers.

Using the app

Which schools and regions are covered?

248 selective schools: grammar schools across England, partially selective schools, and Northern Ireland's SEAG schools. Each entry knows which subjects that school tests, so practice stays focused on what your child will actually sit.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. The app is designed for both, including full support for iPad layouts in portrait and landscape — and the working-out notepad gets a bigger canvas on iPad.

Does my child need an account or an internet connection?

No accounts at all, and once installed the app works fully offline. All data stays on the device — see our privacy policy.

Is it suitable for children to use on their own?

Yes — the child experience is friendly, self-contained and free of external links, ads and chat. Settings, data and anything parent-facing sit behind a PIN-protected Parent Mode. The kids' guide shows exactly what they see.

Can they show their working out, like on paper?

Yes. Maths quizzes and mocks have a floating squared-paper notepad — your child drags a little Taco bubble anywhere on screen, taps it, and draws their working with a finger. Each new question starts on a fresh page.

Will it send my child notifications?

Only if you switch them on. Practice reminders are set up by you in Parent Mode, delivered as local notifications at times you choose, and there's a Holiday Mode that pauses reminders and protects the streak while you're away. Everything can be turned off in one tap.

How do mock tests work?

Real-format, timed tests of 20–50 questions in any subject. Taco's help is always off during mocks, so scores are honest. You get per-subject best and average scores, a full per-question report for every attempt, and a readiness trend once there's enough evidence.

Can my child personalise the app?

They can pick one of six colour themes for their screens, and earn XP to unlock new looks for Taco — Thinker, Scholar, Champion and Superstar. Each child's choices are their own. There's nothing to buy inside the child's app; everything is earned with practice.

Plans & billing

How much does it cost?

Every new family gets a 3-day free trial with full access. After that it's £8.99 a month (or £49.99 a year) for one child, and £11.99 a month (or £64.99 a year) for the Family plan with separate profiles for two or more children. See pricing for what's included.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your subscription starts automatically unless you cancel before the 3 days are up — Apple sends the standard reminders, and you can cancel any time in your App Store settings. Your child's progress is never deleted either way.

What's the difference between Solo and Family?

Both include everything the app can do. Family adds separate profiles for each of your children — their own plans, progress, mistakes, rewards, colour themes and streaks — with one-tap switching in Parent Mode.

How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by the App Store: cancel any time in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions, and you keep access until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Parents & privacy

How much can I control as a parent?

A lot — it's your app. You set the help level Taco offers (all help, one clue only, or none), daily question targets, assignments with their own rules, reminders, real-world rewards and mock scheduling. The parent guide walks through every screen.

Where does my child's data go?

Nowhere. Profiles, progress, mistakes and rewards live only on your device — no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data sold, ever. Payment is handled by the App Store, so we never see payment details either. Full details in the privacy policy.
