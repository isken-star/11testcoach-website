Meet the coach

This is Taco. The whole app is named after him.

Every child gets stuck. What happens in that moment decides whether they learn something or quietly give up — so we built a coach for it. Taco sits on every one of the 12,987 questions in the app, and he has never once given away an answer.

He nudges. He doesn't tell. A child who is stuck gets a way in, tries again themselves, and gets the credit — while you get an honest record of how often the help was needed.

Stuck on a question? Taco helps one small step at a time.

Your child chooses how much help to take. Each step gives a little more — and the answer is never one of them.

🤫Never the answerEvery hint is checked automatically against the correct answer before it ships. If a hint gives the game away, it doesn't ship.
⏱️Silent in mock testsTaco is switched off in every timed mock, so the scores you use to judge readiness are honestly your child's own.
⭐️Rewards going it aloneAnswering correctly without opening Taco earns a bonus. Beating a question they previously got wrong pays full marks too.
🔒 In Parent Mode

See exactly how much help your child is really using

This is the part tutors can't give you. Parent Mode counts every hint, so you know whether your child is flying or quietly leaning on Taco to get through — and it separates the two accuracies that matter:

  • Accuracy without help — what they can do alone today. This is the number that has to rise before the exam.
  • Accuracy after a hint — how they do on the questions they found hard. A gap between the two isn't bad news; it's your revision list.
  • Topics where help was used most — ranked, so you can see that fractions needed six nudges this week and geometry needed none.

You also choose how much help exists in the first place: all of Taco's help, one clue only, or none at all — per child, and separately for homework you set them.

🎛️ Your controls

You decide how much of a safety net Taco is

Some children need Taco every day at first and less as they grow into the work. Others need him switched off to stop the shortcut. It's your call, and you can change it any time.

You control: whether Taco appears in free practice at all, the default help level for assignments you set, read-aloud for children who prefer listening, the independent-answer XP bonus, and Taco's voice. Full walkthrough in the parent guide.

Real screens, straight from the app. One question screen per subject, plus the home screen your child starts from and the progress views you get as a parent.

🔢 Maths

5,200+ questions, from quick arithmetic to full word problems

Every answer is marked instantly and explained step by step, so a wrong answer becomes a small lesson rather than a red cross. Difficulty adapts topic by topic as your child improves — and a floating squared-paper notepad gives them somewhere to do their working out, just like on paper.

Covers: number & place value, addition & subtraction, multiplication & division, fractions, decimals & percentages, money, time, measurement, geometry, number sequences, data & charts, and word problems.

📖 English

2,900+ questions built around 196 original reading passages

Comprehension works the way the exam does: read a full passage, then answer questions on it — retrieval, inference and deduction, not just word-spotting. The passage stays one tap away while your child answers, and grammar, punctuation, spelling and cloze exercises round out the paper.

Covers: reading comprehension, inference & deduction, grammar, punctuation, spelling, synonyms & antonyms, vocabulary in context, cloze passages, sentence completion, literary devices, and summarising.

💬 Verbal Reasoning

2,400+ questions across 15 exam question types

VR is the paper most children have never seen before — so the app teaches the technique, not just the answer. Each explanation shows the pattern behind the question, and Taco can offer a nudge (or stay silent — you choose) while your child learns codes, analogies and sequences.

Covers: letter & number codes, letter sequences, word analogies, hidden words, compound words, odd one out, synonyms & antonyms, missing letters, sentence logic, and ordering.

🧩 Non-Verbal Reasoning

2,300+ questions across 18 spatial question types

Crisp, zoomable diagrams for the puzzles that decide NVR papers — nets and cubes, paper folding, hole punching, rotations. Every answer comes with a visual explanation of why, so your child builds the spatial habits examiners test rather than guessing at shapes.

Covers: nets & cubes, paper folding, hole punching, rotations, reflections, symmetry, matrices, shape sequences, shape codes, embedded shapes, block patterns, and 3-D views.

Every subject also has real-format timed mock tests, a mistakes bank that rebuilds practice around what your child got wrong, and daily plans that mix all of it in the right proportions.

Our story

Built at home, for our daughter Taira.

That's us — my wife, me, and our daughter Taira.

Halfway through Year 5 we found out about the 11+ — and that the exam comes round just weeks into Year 6. What we found to help her prepare was either expensive, stuck on a desktop computer, or a pile of papers we'd have to work through ourselves.

So I built what we needed. Over the past six months I've learned the exams inside out, written and checked every question, and made 11+ Test Coach — first for Taira, then for her friends and their classmates. They've tried every screen and told me, bluntly the way children do, what worked and what didn't.

Two things mattered most. Practice had to be fun, because a child who enjoys it will do a little every day — and a little every day is what actually works. And Parent Mode, which I'll admit I built for myself: I wanted to open the app and see at a glance how she's doing in each subject, and exactly which weak areas we should work on together.

Taira is practising on it right now, with her test this September. I hope it helps your family too.

— Iskender, Taira's dad, and the person who built the app

Common questions

Quick answers.

Using the app

Which schools and regions are covered?

248 selective schools: grammar schools across England, partially selective schools, and Northern Ireland's SEAG schools. Each entry knows which subjects that school tests, so practice stays focused on what your child will actually sit.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. The app is designed for both, including full support for iPad layouts in portrait and landscape — and the working-out notepad gets a bigger canvas on iPad.

Does my child need an account or an internet connection?

No accounts at all, and once installed the app works fully offline. All data stays on the device — see our privacy policy.

Is it suitable for children to use on their own?

Yes — the child experience is friendly, self-contained and free of external links, ads and chat. Settings, data and anything parent-facing sit behind a PIN-protected Parent Mode. The kids' guide shows exactly what they see.

Can they show their working out, like on paper?

Yes. Maths and Verbal Reasoning quizzes and mocks have a floating squared-paper notepad — your child drags a little Taco bubble anywhere on screen, taps it, and draws their working with a finger. It's just as useful for letter codes and alphabet positions as it is for long multiplication. Each new question starts on a fresh page.

Will it send my child notifications?

Only if you switch them on. Practice reminders are set up by you in Parent Mode, delivered as local notifications at times you choose, and there's a Holiday Mode that pauses reminders and protects the streak while you're away. Everything can be turned off in one tap.

How do mock tests work?

Real-format, timed tests of 20–50 questions in any subject. Taco's help is always off during mocks, so scores are honest. You get per-subject best and average scores, a full per-question report for every attempt, and a readiness trend once there's enough evidence.

Does Taco have a hint for every question?

Yes — every single one. All 12,987 active questions carry two hints, a worked example on a different problem, and the key skill being tested, so a child who taps "Give me a clue" is never met with an empty screen. It's enforced automatically: if a question were ever added without coaching, our build would fail. See how the hints work →

Won't Taco just tell my child the answer?

No. Hints point at the method, never the solution, and every hint is checked automatically against its question's correct answer before release — anything that gives the game away is rejected. Taco is also completely silent during timed mock tests, and answering without opening him earns bonus XP. If you'd still rather limit him, Parent Mode offers one clue only or no help at all per child.

Why does Taco's read-aloud voice sound robotic?

Because it runs on your device, not in the cloud. Apps with lifelike AI voices send the text to a company's servers every time they speak — for us that would mean your child's questions and answers leaving your phone, which we won't do. iPhones only pre-install a basic voice, but Apple's Premium ones are free: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, download a British Premium voice, then pick it in Parent Mode → Manage Child → Taco Help → Taco's voice. One download, still fully offline. Full walkthrough →

How does XP work — can my child game it?

No. Answering a question earns 1 XP for effort, but right answers earn 6–13× more (scaled by difficulty, plus a no-hints bonus). Beating a question they got wrong earlier pays the full bonus — that's learning — while re-answering ones they already got right pays nothing. Mocks scale with length and accuracy. The full table is in the parent guide.

Can my child personalise the app?

They can pick one of six colour themes for their screens, and earn XP to unlock new looks for Taco — Thinker, Scholar, Champion and Superstar. Each child's choices are their own. There's nothing to buy inside the child's app; everything is earned with practice.
