Halfway through Year 5 we found out about the 11+ — and that the exam comes round just weeks into Year 6. What we found to help her prepare was either expensive, stuck on a desktop computer, or a pile of papers we'd have to work through ourselves.

So I built what we needed. Common questions

Quick answers.

Using the app

Which schools and regions are covered?

248 selective schools: grammar schools across England, partially selective schools, and Northern Ireland's SEAG schools. Each entry knows which subjects that school tests, so practice stays focused on what your child will actually sit.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. The app is designed for both, including full support for iPad layouts in portrait and landscape — and the working-out notepad gets a bigger canvas on iPad.

Does my child need an account or an internet connection?

No accounts at all, and once installed the app works fully offline. All data stays on the device — see our privacy policy.

Is it suitable for children to use on their own?

Yes — the child experience is friendly, self-contained and free of external links, ads and chat. Settings, data and anything parent-facing sit behind a PIN-protected Parent Mode. The kids' guide shows exactly what they see.

Can they show their working out, like on paper?

Yes. Maths and Verbal Reasoning quizzes and mocks have a floating squared-paper notepad — your child drags a little Taco bubble anywhere on screen, taps it, and draws their working with a finger. It's just as useful for letter codes and alphabet positions as it is for long multiplication. Each new question starts on a fresh page.

Will it send my child notifications?

Only if you switch them on. Practice reminders are set up by you in Parent Mode, delivered as local notifications at times you choose, and there's a Holiday Mode that pauses reminders and protects the streak while you're away. Everything can be turned off in one tap.

How do mock tests work?

Real-format, timed tests of 20–50 questions in any subject. Taco's help is always off during mocks, so scores are honest. You get per-subject best and average scores, a full per-question report for every attempt, and a readiness trend once there's enough evidence.

Does Taco have a hint for every question?

Yes — every single one. Every active question carries two hints, a worked example on a different problem, and the key skill being tested, so a child who taps "Give me a clue" is never met with an empty screen. It's enforced automatically: if a question were ever added without coaching, our build would fail. See how the hints work →

Won't Taco just tell my child the answer?

No. Hints point at the method, never the solution, and every hint is checked automatically against its question's correct answer before release — anything that gives the game away is rejected. Taco is also completely silent during timed mock tests, and answering without opening him earns bonus XP. If you'd still rather limit him, Parent Mode offers one clue only or no help at all per child.

How does XP work — can my child game it?

No. Answering a question earns 1 XP for effort, but right answers earn 6–13× more (scaled by difficulty, plus a no-hints bonus). Beating a question they got wrong earlier pays the full bonus — that's learning — while re-answering ones they already got right pays nothing. Mocks scale with length and accuracy. The full table is in the parent guide.

Can my child personalise the app?

They can pick one of six colour themes for their screens, and earn XP to unlock new looks for Taco — Thinker, Scholar, Champion and Superstar. Each child's choices are their own. There's nothing to buy inside the child's app; everything is earned with practice.

Yes, with the Family plan. It's enabled for Apple Family Sharing, so one purchase unlocks the app on each child's own iPad, under their own Apple ID, for up to six people in your Apple family group.
