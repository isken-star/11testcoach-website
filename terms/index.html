11+ Test Coach provides educational practice content for children preparing for UK 11+ entrance assessments. By using the app you agree to these terms.

A learning aid, not a guarantee

Practice questions, mock tests, baseline assessments and progress insights are learning aids. They are designed to build skill and confidence, but they do not guarantee exam results or school admission outcomes, and mock scores are not predictions of real exam performance.

School information

The built-in database of selective schools (test providers, subjects tested and selection policies) is compiled from publicly available sources and can change at any time. Always confirm entrance test details directly with the school or your local admissions authority before making decisions.

For families

The app is intended to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian. Parent Mode is protected by the PIN you choose during setup — please keep it private from your child. You are responsible for the accuracy of any reward goals you promise!

Purchases, free trial and billing

11+ Test Coach is bought through Apple's App Store, either as a one-off pass or as an auto-renewing subscription:

  • Exam Year Pass — £99.99 as a single payment, giving one child profile 12 months of full access. It does not renew automatically. Access ends 12 months after purchase unless you choose to buy another pass; you will not be charged again and there is nothing to cancel.
  • Family Pass — £149.99 as a single payment, giving every child profile in the household 12 months of full access, on the same non-renewing terms. Family plans are enabled for Apple Family Sharing, so the purchase unlocks the app for members of your Apple family group on their own devices; if sharing is later turned off or you leave the family group, access on those devices ends.
  • Monthly — £16.99 per month for one child profile, or £24.99 per month for every child in the household. These renew automatically until cancelled.

The 7-day free trial applies to the monthly plans. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the monthly subscription starts automatically and payment is charged to your Apple ID account; it then renews at the end of each period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours beforehand. You can manage or cancel a monthly subscription at any time in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, and cancelling keeps access until the end of the current paid period.

Refunds are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency at the time of purchase and may change for future purchases or billing periods with notice via the App Store; a change never affects a pass you have already bought. Your child's data stays on your device regardless of purchase status.
