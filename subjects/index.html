🔢 Maths

5,100+ questions, from quick arithmetic to full word problems

Every answer is marked instantly and explained step by step, so a wrong answer becomes a small lesson rather than a red cross. Difficulty adapts topic by topic as your child improves — and a floating squared-paper notepad gives them somewhere to do their working out, just like on paper.

Covers: number & place value, addition & subtraction, multiplication & division, fractions, decimals & percentages, money, time, measurement, geometry, number sequences, data & charts, and word problems.

📖 English

2,700+ questions built around 196 original reading passages

Comprehension works the way the exam does: read a full passage, then answer questions on it — retrieval, inference and deduction, not just word-spotting. The passage stays one tap away while your child answers, and grammar, punctuation, spelling and cloze exercises round out the paper.

Covers: reading comprehension, inference & deduction, grammar, punctuation, spelling, synonyms & antonyms, vocabulary in context, cloze passages, sentence completion, literary devices, and summarising.

💬 Verbal Reasoning

2,300+ questions across 15 exam question types

VR is the paper most children have never seen before — so the app teaches the technique, not just the answer. Each explanation shows the pattern behind the question, and Taco can offer a nudge (or stay silent — you choose) while your child learns codes, analogies and sequences.

Covers: letter & number codes, letter sequences, word analogies, hidden words, compound words, odd one out, synonyms & antonyms, missing letters, sentence logic, and ordering.

🧩 Non-Verbal Reasoning

2,100+ questions across 18 spatial question types

Crisp, zoomable diagrams for the puzzles that decide NVR papers — nets and cubes, paper folding, hole punching, rotations. Every answer comes with a visual explanation of why, so your child builds the spatial habits examiners test rather than guessing at shapes.

Covers: nets & cubes, paper folding, hole punching, rotations, reflections, symmetry, matrices, shape sequences, shape codes, embedded shapes, block patterns, and 3-D views.

Every subject also has real-format timed mock tests, a mistakes bank that rebuilds practice around what your child got wrong, and daily plans that mix all of it in the right proportions.
